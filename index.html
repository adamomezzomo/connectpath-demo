The mechanism

The Embedded Hiring Engine.

Why this works when everything you've already tried failed. Four pillars, none of which a traditional agency can copy.

01

Compounding AI sourcing

Proprietary AI sources qualified candidates at a speed manual recruiting can't touch, and it gets smarter with every search. An agency's sourcing is the same on day 1,000 as day one. Ours compounds.

02

Human vetting layer

AI finds them. A senior recruiter vets them. Every candidate is screened and interviewed against a role-specific rubric before you ever see them. You get a shortlist, not a resume pile.

03

Culture immersion

We spend time inside your company, meeting your team and learning how you actually work, so the engineers we send fit the way you build. And stay.

04

Aligned flat fee

We're paid the same whether your engineer earns $200k or $400k. Zero incentive to inflate salaries. We optimize for best fit, fastest close, most cost-effective offer.
