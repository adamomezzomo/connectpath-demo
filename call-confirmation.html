Before our call

Here are some of our FAQs.

What actually happens on this call?
Thirty minutes with Alland. We pressure-test the role, comp, and timeline, then tell you honestly whether the embedded model is the right call. No hard pitch, no slide deck.
Why is it $25,000?
Flat, one time, per search, and $40,000 for leadership roles above $400k comp. Never a percentage, so we don't profit from inflating your offer. It's cheaper than a contingency agency on most senior roles, and a rounding error next to a bad hire.
What if you don't deliver?
A triple guarantee: qualified candidates in 14 days or we keep working at no cost, a hire in 30 days or a full refund, and a free 90-day replacement. First hire in 21 days, or you don't pay.
We've used a recruiter before and it went badly.
Most founders have: resume dumps, months of drift, comp inflation. We embed in your team and vet every candidate against your rubric before you see them. Ask us on the call about the searches that didn't go perfectly, too.
How is 21 days actually possible?
15 to 25 vetted profiles in week one from AI sourcing across the right pools, then vetting, your interviews, and close on a fixed sequence. It's a process, not a promise pulled from thin air.
What do you actually need from my team?
A decision-maker, aligned comp and requirements, and fast feedback. That's what keeps the 21-day clock honest. We handle sourcing, screening, scheduling, and offer support.
Our role is too niche for this.
Niche is where targeted sourcing beats job boards by the widest margin. The people you want aren't applying anywhere; they have to be found and approached directly. That's the whole system.
What if we're not a fit?
Then we'll say so on the call and point you somewhere better. We cap active searches and only take work we can guarantee, so a clean ‘not now’ is a fine outcome.
