Why we exist

Doing it yourself and hiring a percentage agency both fail founders. So we built a third way.

Process

Your path to the hire.

Five phases, one clock. Founder responsiveness on interviews and feedback keeps it on rails.

Step 01

Intake & calibration

Kickoff call, role alignment, comp review. We build a role-specific scoring rubric with your hiring team and immerse in your culture on-site.

Step 02

Sourcing & outreach

Compounding AI sourcing plus conversion-optimized outreach to the high-quality passive candidates who never apply to job posts.

Step 03

Vetting & shortlist

Senior recruiter screen and first-round interview before you see anyone. Candidates scored against your weighted rubric, delivered as a weekly shortlist.

Step 04

Founder interviews

We run all scheduling and coordination, keep structured feedback loops with your team, and flag standouts in real time.

Step 05

Offer & close

Offer guidance and compensation intelligence so you attract without overpaying, with support through to acceptance.

Always on

Full transparency

Weekly update call plus a live progress dashboard: sourced, contacted, responded, qualified, in-process. You always know what's happening next.
