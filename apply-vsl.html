Straight answers

What founders ask before they book.

How is 21 days actually possible?
We don't start sourcing cold. The Embedded Hiring Engine runs AI sourcing across the exact talent pools that matter and delivers 15 to 25 vetted profiles in week one, so your interviews start while other agencies are still writing the job description. Intake days 1 to 2, sourcing days 2 to 7, vetting days 7 to 12, your interviews days 12 to 19, offer and close days 20 to 21.
What does it cost, and why a flat fee?
A flat $25,000 per search, or $40,000 for leadership roles compensated above $400k. One time, per search, never a percentage. A percentage fee pays the recruiter more when your hire costs more, which quietly works against you at the offer table. Flat fee keeps us on your side of it.
We've used recruiters before and got burned.
Most founders have: resume dumps, months of drift, and a model that profits from inflating comp. We embed inside your team, screen and interview every candidate against your rubric before you ever see them, and back the whole thing with a triple guarantee. Different model, different result.
What if the hire doesn't work out?
You're covered three ways: qualified candidates in 14 days or we keep working at no cost, a hire in 30 days or a full refund, and a free 90-day replacement search if a placed candidate leaves. And the headline promise holds: first hire in 21 days, or you don't pay.
Why pay upfront instead of only on a hire?
The engagement fee is a filter. It means we only take founders serious enough to move, so we can cap active searches and give each one real attention. It also funds the sourcing that happens in week one, before a contingency recruiter would lift a finger, and it's fully guaranteed, so the risk sits with us, not you.
What roles do you fill?
Senior and staff software engineers first, plus engineering leadership (CTO, VP Eng, EM) and key operators (Head of Staff, Finance, GTM). US and Canada, roughly the $200k to $550k+ band, for venture-backed AI and software startups from Pre-Seed to Series A.
What do you need from our team?
A clear decision-maker, aligned comp and role requirements, and fast feedback on candidates and interviews. The 21-day clock depends on founder responsiveness. We handle the sourcing, screening, scheduling, and offer support.
We're not hiring this exact second.
Then this may not be the moment, and we'll tell you so honestly. We work best when hiring is a live priority with budget approved. If you're a quarter out, book anyway and we'll map the search so you can move the day you're ready.
